My elevator pitch

I’m Aidan Scott, a cybersecurity student at the University of Cincinnati and a full-time IT Specialist I at Root Insurance. My experience includes troubleshooting Windows and Mac computers, working with Microsoft 365 and ServiceNow, and helping with networking issues. I enjoy figuring out why something isn’t working and explaining the solution clearly. I’m building on that experience through my coursework and hands-on learning, with the goal of moving into cybersecurity and helping organizations keep their systems reliable and secure.

A little more about me

My background combines classroom learning with practical IT work. Before my current role, I worked as an IT Help Desk Intern at Root Insurance and a Level 1 Technician at Next Level Technologies. These experiences helped me develop a patient, organized approach to troubleshooting and user support.

I’m interested in how networks, devices, accounts, and people fit together. I want to keep strengthening my technical foundation while learning to recognize and reduce security risks.
